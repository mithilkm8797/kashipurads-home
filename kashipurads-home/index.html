Personal Vision

Crafting a sanctuary in black, white, wood & gold.

For Mithil, a home is more than physical walls—it is a personal sanctuary combining the stark refinement of matte black and crisp porcelain white with the grounding warmth of rich natural walnut wood.

Every spatial reconfiguration across all 7 specialized zones was curated to balance grand architectural statements with intimate, meditative corners illuminated by champagne gold fixtures.
